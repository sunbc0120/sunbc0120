### What I Do

I architect and prototype enterprise AI systems across distributed post-training, multimodal agents, and regulated workflows. I work across the full decision chain: frame the business and technical decision, build the critical path, demonstrate it under real constraints, and hand over something the customer team can operate.

My goal is not to choose between engineering and strategy. It is to use technical depth to improve consequential decisions—and to stay close enough to implementation to know when the slide deck is wrong.

- **Build:** Own the technically uncertain path from architecture to working proof.
- **Decide:** Connect model and platform choices to economics, security, governance, and organisational readiness.
- **Multiply:** Turn individual engagements into reusable architectures, public knowledge, and stronger delivery teams.

### Selected Field Work

- **Regulated Financial Services:** Architected and built an agentic workflow and multimodal OCR pipeline for mortgage approvals at a global financial institution. Worked through regulatory, privacy, and data-governance constraints to deliver a production-oriented prototype and help secure a multi-million-dollar competitive cloud AI program.

- **Deadline-Critical Accelerated Compute:** Recovered a high-stakes migration for a large regional enterprise by refactoring a research-heavy computer-vision stack and deploying its core transformer workloads on Google Kubernetes Engine with NVIDIA H200 GPUs.

- **Reinforcement Learning at Enterprise Scale:** Led the architecture and deployment of Gemma 3 RL fine-tuning with NeMo-RL on enterprise GKE H200/B200 clusters. Resolved critical integration dependencies in time for an NVIDIA GTC 2026 showcase.

- **Security and Sovereignty by Design:** Embedded with a leading ANZ digital-native company to design the foundation of its corporate AI assistant. Built custom Infrastructure-as-Code controls for Customer-Managed Encryption Key rotation, closing platform gaps while satisfying stringent InfoSec and data-sovereignty requirements.

- **Reusable Multimodal Applications:** Built a scalable Generative UI framework with bidirectional audio streaming and Voice Activity Detection for a leading APAC aviation company. Generalised the implementation into a reusable regional reference architecture for faster agentic-AI delivery.

### How I Operate

- **Under pressure:** Management consulting taught me to remain useful when the room becomes difficult—turn conflict into decisions, convert ambiguity into an executable plan, and stay accountable through delivery rather than stopping at the presentation.

- **Without a playbook:** Research taught me to be comfortable with problems I have not solved before: form hypotheses, build experiments, and converge on a workable answer under real constraints.

- **With ownership:** I architect, prototype, build, demonstrate, and hand over. The work spans executive stakeholders, platform teams, researchers, and the engineers who must operate the result.
